From first setup to your next attempt

  1. 1

    Set up your interview

    Choose the interview type, level, language and length. Add the job description so every question is about the job you want.

  2. 2

    Fix your CV first

    Upload your CV and paste the job description. See what matches, what is missing, and get a stronger version to send.

  3. 3

    Answer in the interview room

    Questions appear one at a time with a timer. Speak your answer the way you would on the day, then move on.

  4. 4

    Read your report, then go again

    Get a score for each skill, written AI feedback on every answer, and a new interview scheduled when you are ready.
